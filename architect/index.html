The six-part engagement

From “I want to” to “here is the plan.”

The subject changes. The architecture stays consistent. Every engagement turns a broad ambition into a defined outcome, a sequence of work, and visible proof of progress.

01

Discover

Intake, starting point, context, strengths, constraints, stakeholders, and deadline.

02

Define

One primary goal, the 90-day result, success measures, and what is explicitly out of scope.

03

Design

Milestones, weekly actions, dependencies, decisions, resources, and creative direction.

04

Build

The roadmap, deliverable list, calendar, scorecard, and briefs needed to begin execution.

05

Activate

A working session that assigns owners, schedules the first moves, and removes ambiguity.

06

Review

Progress review, course correction, next-phase recommendation, and decision on support.

The Architect creates strategy, structure, creative direction, and implementation roadmaps. It does not promise an agency contract, income, investment, public funding, health outcome, or government adoption.

  • No medical diagnosis or treatment
  • No legal, tax, investment, or regulated professional advice
  • No guaranteed bookings, representation, grants, sales, or partnerships
  • No production work unless included in the written scope

Intake

Ten questions that begin the architecture.

You do not need polished answers. You do need a real ambition and enough honesty to describe the starting point.

What are you trying to become, build, launch, change, or improve?

What would a meaningful result look like 90 days from now?

Why does this matter now?

What have you already tried, created, or committed?

What is the biggest constraint: clarity, skill, time, money, access, confidence, or coordination?

Who must participate, approve, support, or benefit?

What deadline, event, season, or opportunity shapes the timing?

What assets, relationships, experience, or resources already exist?

What kind of support do you need: plan, creative direction, accountability, production, or program design?

What budget and weekly time can you realistically commit?
